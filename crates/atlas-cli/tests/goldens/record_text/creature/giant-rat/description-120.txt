Giant Rat
  Type: creature · Pack: Monster Core
  Key: pathfinder-monster-core:iIJPJcDT8wlJ8z5M

Public Notes
  Giant rats are enormous versions of the common vermin. They are typically found in abundant numbers, but since they
  cannot fit in the nooks where mundane rats typically hide, they are much easier to locate and exterminate. They mostly
  live in sewers where they can scavenge from the streets above, but some families of giant rats live in more remote
  locations, such as dank caves, forests or hills. Rats are incredibly adept survivors and can be found nearly anywhere
  in the world, though they tend to favor temperate or warm climates as opposed to cold regions.
  Although its bite alone is not lethal except to the very young or very old, the giant rat carries the putrid plague
  common to rodents around the world—a pestilence more than capable of ravaging rural communities.
  ---
  Rats are a ubiquitous menace, scurrying through the sewers and on the streets of nearly every settlement in the world.
  Though a regular rat darting underfoot might startle or even frighten the average passerby, giant rats and rat swarms
  are far more dangerous.

Putrid Plague
  The Sickened and Unconscious conditions from putrid plague don't improve on their own until the disease is cured.
  Saving Throw Fortitude DC 14
  ---
  Stage 1 carrier with no ill effect (1d4 hours)
  Stage 2 Sickened 1 (1 day)
  Stage 3 Sickened 1 and Slowed 1 (1 day)
  Stage 4 Unconscious (1 day)
  Stage 5 dead
