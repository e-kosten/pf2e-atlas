Public Notes
  This huge forge or oven is animated to protect a workspace or kitchen, travel alongside its owner, or both.
  ---
  Many animated objects have useful functions but become dangers when uncontrolled.

Summary
  Weak animated furnace

Construct Armor (Hardness 8)
  Like normal objects, an animated furnace has Hardness. This Hardness reduces any damage the furnace takes by an amount
  equal to the Hardness. Once an animated furnace is reduced to fewer than half its Hit Points, or immediately upon
  being damaged by a critical hit, its construct armor breaks, removing the Hardness and reducing its Armor Class to 24.

Fan the Flames
  The animated furnace opens its door and fans its flames in a cone that deals 5d6[fire] damage (Reflex DC 28 save).
