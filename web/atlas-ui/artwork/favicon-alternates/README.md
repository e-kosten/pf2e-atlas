# Favicon artwork

The active favicon is [Revised die faces — Parchment + oxblood](../../public/favicon.svg).
It combines a four-point compass with a projected d20 interior and upper-left
lighting. Its transparent canvas fits the artwork without changing its proportions.

These matching parchment + oxblood alternatives are retained for future selection:

- [Ornate spellbook · compass rose](ornate-spellbook-compass-parchment-oxblood.svg):
  a vector reconstruction of spellbook concept 12, with a compass cover emblem,
  metal corners and clasp, without a bookmark.
- [Detailed reference](detailed-compass-rose-parchment-oxblood.svg): the detailed
  faceted compass rose, locally traced from the supplied PNG and recolored as SVG.

The alternatives are not imported by the app and are outside `public` and `src`,
so they are not included in the web build. All three designs retain the reviewed
SVG geometry, palette and fitted canvas; no bitmap is embedded.

To choose an alternative later, replace `public/favicon.svg` with the selected SVG
and rebuild the frontend. Keep the alternative here as the review reference.
