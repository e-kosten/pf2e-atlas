`LoadingStatus` is a shared Ant composition, mounted only while loading. It reserves its slot with visibility rather than adding delayed layout growth. Unmounting cancels its timer. It does not cache data, delay error reporting or authorize actions.

## Existing patterns retained

- `RecordDetailPane`, shared reference reading, trait reading and encounter authored-text reading already retain same-source content with delayed, out-of-flow refresh indicators and explicit generation retirement.
- `SearchPickerModal` prepares an explicit candidate before switching its reader and delays preparation feedback. It keeps error recovery local.
- `InitiativePopover` waits for options or a failure before opening and delays feedback on the initiating control.
- `useConditionEventPreview` and encounter-add difficulty previews retain a matching form subject while preventing stale submission.
- Turn advance prepares its reminder boundary before revealing it. Persistent review and lifecycle queries scope their retained placeholders to encounter, participant and boundary.
- Filter skeletons occupy the eventual control slots; they are not replacement content for an already displayed document. Immediate disabling of an unsafe action remains independent of delayed visual progress.

## Remaining audit findings

These are separate from the repaired strike/read-continuity defects and have not been represented as fixed:

1. Several mutation buttons still use immediate boolean `loading`: HP/condition/spell/resource saves, persistent recovery, list edits/removal, party editors and modal submissions. These keep drafts/content, but fast writes may flash a spinner or change the button footprint. A follow-up should delay visual busy feedback while retaining an immediate explicit pending guard; changing the spinner alone can accidentally permit duplicate input. Relevant owners include `EncounterHpEditor`, `EncounterConditionEditor`, `EncounterSpellControls`, `EncounterPersistentRecovery`, `ListEntryNote`, `CreateListModal` and shared `DangerActionButton`.
2. `EncounterLifeStatus` initially derives its split-button shape from an empty lifecycle read, and `EncounterConditionLifecycle` derives secondary actions from asynchronously loaded choices. A cold read can reveal controls later. Review a reserved control footprint or preparation before disclosure; do not invent unavailable actions or preload every participant unconditionally.

The encounter follow-up owner is [Encounter runner usability follow-through](../../backlog/items/rust-web-encounter-runner-polish.md).

## Evidence and limits

- Existing strike/dice tests plus readiness, refresh/failure retention, identity isolation and focus regressions passed. Shared delayed-status and Drained preview regressions passed.
- Focused related-record, embedded-reading, roll-table, search-picker and saved-list tests passed.
- In a full-corpus desktop browser with a forced three-second context delay, pending and slow result views both measured 169.5 px high. The ready context expanded once to its intentional 520 × 560 px footprint and focused the dialog. The injected delay was removed after the check.
- Human early UX feedback: Looks stable; continue.
- Independent Astra Chromium review found no remaining blockers at desktop (1200 × 897, dark), phone emulation (390 × 844, dark/reduced motion) and intermediate (768 × 700, light/reduced motion) sizes. It confirmed ready-before-expand behavior, delayed feedback, local failure/Retry, focus on expansion and Back, and no page overflow. Removing Retry after successful recovery can leave focus on the document body until the next Tab. Full frontend gate and device acceptance are recorded in the task/PR after completion. Source inspection is not a claim that every loading transition received slow-network browser coverage. Physical devices, software keyboards and real text zoom require separate evidence.
