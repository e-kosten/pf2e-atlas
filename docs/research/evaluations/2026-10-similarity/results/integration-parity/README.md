This is a separate, bounded post-study comparison of the historical generation-6
artifact and the task-local generation-7 replacement, using the immutable product
CLI at revision `0ed3dd72dd1414492ddce10b4824d46ed3291c60` (SHA256
`453eea2d6a099fb431e213b95af1e1493ff3f46b2bcf687f9b9f018602b09e7c`).
It does not amend the frozen quality experiment, establish full-corpus parity, or
measure the eventual final product binary.

Original summary and input-manifest bytes preserve the literal commands, capture
hashes, selectors, eight observed top-twenty lists, exposed scores, seven inventory
checks, and three stale-request responses. Their local raw-capture paths are
historical provenance, not dependencies of recorded-data replay. Full captures
contain game-source bodies and remain in the protected local research archive.
Baseline product output has no numeric score; only its ordering is compared.
Recorded timing was collected during concurrent validation and is diagnostic only.

Native-input metadata binds the changed model/input/token identities and old/new
published-vector checksums. Native metrics preserve per-identity finite/dimension,
norm, component-delta, L2-delta and cosine observations. They contain no vectors
or game-source bodies. Replay joins identities and recomputes metric summaries;
re-running the native float32 math still requires both preserved databases and the
pinned sqlite-vec diagnostic whose source/binary hashes are in native-summary.
Checksum or floating-point deltas alone do not establish their cause.

refresh-validation is the original earlier snapshot, before the native numeric
audit and expanded parity run completed; its statements that those checks are
pending are superseded by native-summary and parity, not silently edited.
Final binary, latency and browser acceptance are separate delivery evidence.
