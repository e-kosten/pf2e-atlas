- id: Damage
  label: Damage
  description: Effects whose primary encounter contribution is harming targets rather

- id: Debilitation
  label: Debilitation
  description: Effects that reduce a target's combat effectiveness by imposing harmful
    states, penalties, or action limits. Examples include fear, slowed, stupefied,
    drained, sickened, and action loss.
- id: Protection
  label: Protection
  description: Effects that prevent, reduce, redirect, or absorb harm as their main
    rules function rather than as a character build role. Examples include resistance,
    immunity, mitigation, and warding.
- id: ForcedPosition
  label: Forced Position
  description: Effects that change where a target is located against its will or
    as a tactical consequence. Examples include push, pull, drag, teleport, and forced
    reposition effects.
- id: SensoryControl
  label: Sensory Control
  description: Effects that alter what combatants can perceive, target, communicate,
    or reveal through sensory channels. Examples include sight blocking, concealment,
    silencing, scent denial, and other sensory interference.
