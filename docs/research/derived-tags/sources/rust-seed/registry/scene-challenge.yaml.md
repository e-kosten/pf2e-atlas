id: SceneChallenge
label: Scene Challenge

subgroups:
- id: ResolutionPattern
  label: Resolution Pattern
  description: Scene challenges where the interesting question is how the party figures
    out and executes the right solution path. Examples include layered resolution,

- id: SpatialConstraint
  label: Spatial Constraint
  description: Scene challenges that create pressure by controlling where creatures

    forced crossings, containment, and blocked routes.
- id: EscalationPressure
  label: Escalation Pressure
  description: Scene challenges that become worse, more expensive, or harder to ignore
    as time passes. Examples include reinforcement, pursuit, endurance pressure,
    resource pressure, countdowns, and spreading threats.
- id: EnvironmentalThreat
  label: Environmental Threat
  description: Scene challenges where the place itself creates danger or constrains
    safe action. Examples include flooding chambers, collapsing structures, battlefield
    hazards, wilderness hazards, fire, smoke, and choking terrain.
- id: SocialObstacle
  label: Social Obstacle
  description: Scene challenges where social pressure, institutions, deception, or
    competing agendas are the obstacle to progress. Examples include negotiation
    barriers, deception webs, faction standoffs, public pressure, and civic obstacles.
- id: Mystery
  label: Mystery
  description: Scene challenges where progress depends on interpreting clues and
    resolving uncertainty rather than only overcoming a visible obstacle. Examples
    include clue chains, hidden culprits, false leads, reveals, and investigation
    scenes.
- id: HauntPattern
  label: Haunt Pattern
  description: Scene challenges that use haunt-specific resolution logic or repeating
    spiritual mechanics rather than only spooky theme. Examples include appeasement,
    haunt sources, spiritual repetition, and unresolved death.
