id: StatusEffect
label: Status Effect
description: Conditions and afflictions as problems, progressions, mechanisms, and
  body-state changes.
subgroups:
- id: AfflictionProgression
  label: Affliction Progression
  description: Status problems where the timing, stages, recurrence, or spread of
    the affliction is central to how it plays. Examples include slow burn, staged
    escalation, contagious spread, relapse, and hidden onset.
- id: AfflictionMechanism
  label: Affliction Mechanism
  description: Status problems grouped by the kind of harmful mechanism they use
    rather than by how to cure them. Examples include disease, poison, curse, fear,
    paralysis, and petrification.
- id: MentalState
  label: Mental State
  description: Status effects that primarily change cognition, emotion, attention,
    judgment, or control of the mind. Examples include frightened, confused, controlled,
    fascinated, stupefied, and emotion-based states.
- id: Impairment
  label: Impairment
  description: Status effects that reduce bodily capability, senses, stamina, or
    action quality without necessarily following an affliction track. Examples include
    slowed, clumsy, enfeebled, drained, sickened, blinded, and deafened.
- id: OngoingDamage
  label: Ongoing Damage
  description: Status problems where recurring harm over time is the core retrieval
    value. Examples include persistent damage, bleed, poison ticking damage, and
    other recurring harm.
- id: Transformation
  label: Transformation
  description: Status effects that change a creature's form, body, or physical nature
    in a way that can alter capabilities or identity. Examples include polymorph,
    mutation, body alteration, and form change.
- id: PhysiologyOverride
  label: Physiology Override
  description: Status effects that override ordinary bodily control or biological
    function rather than merely penalizing performance. Examples include petrification,
    possession, paralysis, sleep, and body control.
