subgroups:
- id: OpeningPlay
  label: Opening Play
  description: Records whose value is highest before or at the start of a scene,

- id: SustainedUse
  label: Sustained Use

    turns, or encounters rather than a single decisive moment. Examples include sustained
    stances, repeatable routines, maintenance actions, and attrition plans.
- id: ResourceUse
  label: Resource Use
  description: Records whose retrieval value depends on how they spend, conserve,
    recharge, or trade limited uses. Examples include consumable bursts, daily powers,
    renewable powers, charge-based tools, and expendable setup.
- id: ReactiveUse
  label: Reactive Use
  description: Records used in response to an opponent, hazard, failure, trigger,
    or incoming danger rather than on a proactive schedule. Examples include counterplay,
    interrupts, punishment, emergency responses, and trigger-driven tools.
- id: SetupPayoff
  label: Setup Payoff
  description: Records that become valuable because another action, condition, mark,
    or preparation step creates a later payoff. Examples include combo enablers,
    mark-and-trigger effects, delayed rewards, and preparation-dependent effects.
